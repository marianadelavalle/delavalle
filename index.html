de Lavalle was established in 2025 by Mariana de Lavalle. Rooted in the belief that clothing should be made with care and made to last, the brand produces each piece locally in Peru — by hand, in-house. de Lavalle's collections explore the quiet strength of a simple wardrobe: pieces without excess, cut to outlast seasons and resist the pull of trends.
